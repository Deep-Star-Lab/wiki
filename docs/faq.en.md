# Frequently Asked Questions

**How can I join Deep Star Lab?**
: Applications to join the core team will open soon. Apart from that, anyone who feels part of the community is already considered a member.

**Who can contribute to this wiki?**
: Any community member can open a pull request.

**Why don't you have any earlier activity?**
: Our core team has actually been involved in many projects; you can check their personal profiles. However, the so-called communities and similar empty spaces on the internet pushed us to go public.
