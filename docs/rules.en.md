# Community Rules

Deep Star Lab is an open, independent, production-focused community. These rules exist to protect those
principles; we kept them short so they get read. This is the first version; changes are made by PR like any other page.

## 1. Core principle

Ideas are discussed freely here, but **people** are treated with respect. Technical criticism is encouraged;
personal insults, harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## 2. Communication

- Our common languages are Turkish and English. We are an international community; if a topic concerns everyone, add an English summary.
- Don't hesitate to ask questions. Be patient with newcomers.
- Use the right channel: general chat, project talk and bug reports should not be mixed.

## 3. Attribution and copyright

- Translations and summaries **must credit the original authors and link to the source**. The page byline shows this in a standard format (see the [Contributing Guide](getting-started/contributing.md)).
- Before translating, check the original's license and whether translation is allowed.
- Publishing someone else's work under your own name is forbidden. Cite every source you use.
- Use the [Glossary](resources/glossary.md) in translations for consistent terminology.

## 4. Contribution rules

- Changes are made through pull requests and reviewed by at least one person.
- State *what* and *why* in the PR description. The site must build with `mkdocs build --strict`.
- Every new page gets an English counterpart (`.en.md`); at minimum the title and a summary.
- Before large changes, open an issue to share your idea.

## 5. Leaderboard integrity

- Every contribution is added with **verifiable proof** (PR, page, repository, paper).
- Inflating points, generating automated/fake contributions or claiming someone else's work is forbidden.
- A wrong entry that is noticed is corrected or removed. Objections are raised openly via an issue or PR.
- The scoring rules are on the [Leaderboard](leaderboard.md) page and are the same for everyone.

## 6. Security and privacy

- Never share secrets such as API keys, passwords or tokens; if you leak one by accident, revoke it immediately.
- When releasing datasets and models, check personal data and license status.
- Sharing someone's personal information without consent (doxxing) is forbidden.

## 7. Spam and promotion

No unsolicited advertising, mass invitations or unrelated links. Sharing your own project is welcome as long as
it is relevant to the community and not repeated over and over.

## 8. Uncensored, with limits

Our principle of independence and accessibility means ideas and information circulate freely. Even so,
**illegal content, harassment, exposure of personal information and malware** are outside the community.

## 9. Enforcement

For violations we apply, in order: a verbal warning → temporary suspension → permanent removal; severe violations
may skip steps. You can report a violation to a moderator on Discord or by opening an issue on GitHub.
Decisions can be appealed.
