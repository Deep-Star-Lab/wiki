# Deep Star Lab

**Deep Star Lab** is a community working on AI with a **production-first** mindset.
Here the emphasis is less on model comparisons and more on the math and software behind the work,
open-source tooling, and the solutions our members use in the real world.

!!! tip "What is this wiki?"
    This space gathers getting-started guides, project records, resources and
    community rules in one place.

## Why Deep Star Lab?

- **Production-focused** — real products, not demos.
- **International** — open to everyone in the world.
- **Open collaboration** — anyone can contribute; we stand against monopolization.
- **Independent** — we do not depend on a single central structure, state or company; we work for the uncensored and accessible nature of the internet as a whole, not only of AI.
- **Experience** — our core team has worked through everything from simple neural networks to the foundations of generative models.
